Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data Processing

When you use Document Assistant, your input (document text, uploaded files) is sent to our backend server for processing. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and used solely to generate your requested analysis or document.

2. AI-Powered Results

Document Assistant uses artificial intelligence models to generate results. Your input is forwarded to the AI model as part of the request. The AI processes your data in real time and does not retain it after generating a response.

3. No Persistent Document Storage

Document Assistant does not permanently store your document text, uploaded file content, or generated results on our servers. Input data is processed in memory and discarded after the response is delivered.

4. Local Device Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your language preference (English or Romanian)
  • A stable, anonymous app user identifier (UUID)

This data never leaves your device except as request headers for usage tracking.

5. Server-Side Usage Data

We store the following on our servers to enforce plan limits:

  • Usage counters (number of requests per module)
  • Subscription status and tier information
  • Anonymous user and device identifiers

We do not store document content, file content, or generated results.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive subscription status updates but do not process or store payment information directly.

7. Advertising

The free tier includes advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google's ad network may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

All data transmission between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Document content is processed in isolated, request-scoped operations and is never written to disk on our servers.

9. Children's Privacy

Document Assistant is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use the "Send Feedback" feature in the app.
